# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are restaurant staff working during active service: managers, servers, cashiers, and kitchen operators. They use the system in time-sensitive conditions to move orders through service quickly and accurately.

## Product Purpose

This product helps restaurant teams run day-to-day service operations from one interface. Success means service moves faster with fewer ordering mistakes and less coordination friction between front-of-house and kitchen workflows.

## Positioning

Run your entire restaurant from one simple system.

## Brand Personality

Warm, welcoming, and human.

## Anti-references

Avoid looking like generic SaaS dashboards with heavy card clutter.

## Design Principles

1. Keep service-moving actions obvious and fast to execute.
2. Prioritize clarity over decoration in every operational state.
3. Preserve a human tone while supporting high-tempo workflows.
4. Reduce handoff friction across the order lifecycle.
5. Favor predictable, repeatable interaction patterns over novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No explicit compliance target is defined; use best-effort accessibility as the default baseline.
